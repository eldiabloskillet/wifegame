wifegame

Create four groups of four. Every word is locked behind a mini-game — play it to uncover the word.

How to play

The board hides 16 words that form 4 groups of 4, just like Connections.

Each square is a different mini-game: crossword, Wordle, word search, hangman, Minesweeper and more. Win a game and its word appears on the board. Lose (or give up) and the word is still revealed, but it's marked as failed.

Once four words are uncovered you can select them and Submit a group. You have 4 mistakes for the whole puzzle.

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. It resets at midnight in your time zone, and your progress is saved in this browser until then.
